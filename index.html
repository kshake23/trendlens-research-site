The research loop

Trend intelligence with a human review layer.

TrendLens is designed to make research more disciplined: use permitted sources, preserve timestamps, expose uncertainty, and never confuse search interest with purchase demand.

01

Collect signals

Google Trends RSS and future approved API access provide time-based and geographic search signals. Etsy research uses permitted Open API access when configured.

02

Compare honestly

Historical series, shared-scale comparisons, momentum, seasonality, and geographic patterns are analyzed without inventing search volume or sales figures.

03

Design with restraint

Approved opportunities become original design briefs. The workflow flags IP and policy risks and keeps final judgment with a human reviewer.

What it explores

From early signal to considered product direction.

Trend signals

Historical interest, geography, momentum, seasonality, and relevance filtering.

Marketplace context

Permitted Etsy listing research to understand visible competition—not private Marketplace Insights counts unless officially available.

Original design research

Five distinct concept briefs, print-quality checks, garment mockups, provenance records, and review-ready exports.
